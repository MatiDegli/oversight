# Design System: Oversight

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Signal"**

Oversight has two surfaces with different jobs, and they never borrow each other's materials. The
graph is the machines' surface: it shows what the agents are doing, with nodes that light up, a
change in its agent's color, and rings and a field of dots that glow like an LED panel. It is the
only place where light moves. The panels are the person's surface: the agents panel, review, live
diff and file view are dark and plain. Their transitions report events. They hold text, decisions
and code.

The product is a tool someone operates for long stretches while agents work, and the demo is
recorded on video. So the screen is dense but calm, every color means something, and nothing moves
without a cause. Everything is dark by default, the review panel included,
with a dark Monaco theme. A day mode exists for the submission gallery only (see Colors).

**Precedence.** AGENTS.md wins over this file. The maintainer approved the v4 shell sketches on
2026-10-27: [watch](docs/sketches/v4/watch.png), [focus](docs/sketches/v4/focus.png),
[conversation](docs/sketches/v4/conversation.png), [review](docs/sketches/review/b-dark.png),
[changes](docs/sketches/v4/changes.png), [settings](docs/sketches/v4/settings.png) and
[motion marker](docs/sketches/v4/motion-marker.png). These are the shell target, inside Signal
and with the matte review header retained. This file records their design, not their implementation.
The maintainer approved the life-layer sketches on 2026-10-29:
[rest](docs/sketches/life/rest-contour.png), [rest in day](docs/sketches/life/rest-day.png),
[rest under reduced motion](docs/sketches/life/rest-rm.png),
[merge travels](docs/sketches/life/merge-mid.png) and a
[motion clip](docs/sketches/life/life-v2.webm). They are the target for the rest state and the life
layer, on the same shell. Two flaws are known and are not copied: the light in merge-mid has a hard
top edge, and the clip runs the contour drift and the light fade at 10x, as its label says. Its
"Sketch" strip is mockup only.
Where a sketch disagrees with AGENTS.md, the table of departures records the rule that wins;
contrast failures remain explicit maintainer notes below. Sizes are CSS pixels on a 1920 by 1080
frame, the size the demo is recorded at. Panels scale with the window; type sizes and radii do not.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Near-black ground (#070707), warm white text, two agent colors and two state colors, nothing else.
- One family, Atkinson Hyperlegible, in sentence case.
- Layered dark surfaces with hairline borders; light lives only on the graph.
- Every state is told in words or signs as well as color.
- The shell gives every view a home; opening a panel preserves the graph and its node positions.

## Colors

A black field with warm near-whites, where each agent hue identifies its slot A–F, and
green and red only ever mean passed and failed.

### Primary

- **Agent A orange** (agent-a): Agent A's node borders, letter badges, rings and light. As text (the
  word on a node, a status pill, the letter in a rail avatar) it uses agent-a-text, lighter so
  small text stays readable on near-black (darker in day mode, for the same reason).
- **Agent B blue** (agent-b): the same roles for Agent B, with agent-b-text for text. Orange and
  blue are the pair that people with the common forms of color blindness tell apart best.
- **Agents C–F:** violet C, pink D, sky E and plum F use their own `agent-c` through
  `agent-f` families for the same roles. The letter always carries identity, including for
  color-blind viewers; six hues cannot stay far apart under every color-vision deficiency.
- **Agent tints** (`agent-{a..f}-tint`): the fill behind a letter avatar or an agent's status
  pill. **Agent node fills** (`agent-{a..f}-node` and the slightly darker `-node-new` family): the
  fill of a node with that agent's pending change.

C–F follow the A/B fill recipe: dark tints use graphic RGB at 14% alpha (A 14%, B 16%
remain unchanged); dark node RGB scales the graphic channels so the maximum channel is 30,
and new-node RGB scales to 20, rounded to the nearest integer, both at 95% alpha. Day tints
use graphic RGB at 12% alpha; day node and new-node fills mix 12% and 7% graphic RGB over
`day-card` in sRGB, rounded to the nearest integer. Existing A/B day fills stay unchanged.
The maintainer approved distinct text values on 2026-10-28 at 22:30 ART: dark F text
`#b27ba7` keeps graphic `#ab6e9e`; day D text `#894674` keeps graphic `#a2568a`;
day E text `#255ea1` keeps graphic `#3475c4`. These text variants meet 4.5:1 on
raised surfaces and on the agent tints used by cards, rail avatars and review pills.
F text reaches 5.14:1 on dark raised and 4.65:1 on its tint over card. Day D/E reach
5.08:1 / 5.07:1 on raised and 4.63:1 / 4.65:1 on their tints over shell.
All slots retain the same translucent tint recipe and original node fills. Tints are
used over shell, card, card-end and matte-top; tint over raised is not an intended pair
(D 4.42:1 and E 4.44:1). Approval changes text only, including on the existing node fills;
graphics and filled-badge letters retain their values.

### Secondary

- **Passed green** (passed): passing tests, added lines, the `+` count. Its tint (added-tint) is the
  background of an added line.
- **Failed red** (failed): failing tests, removed lines, the `-` count, Reject, Stop and discard.
  As text it uses failed-text. Its tint (removed-tint) is the background of a removed line.

The neutral roles:

- **Ground** (ground): behind the graph, and the page itself.
- **Shell** (shell): the app frame: top bar, left rail; graph overlays (legend, zoom) at 90%.
- **Panel** (panel): the agents panel; the cone card on the graph at 94%; module frames at 90%.
- **Inset** (inset): inputs and terminal. Field (field) takes the same value for search, task and
  comment boxes.
- **Page** (page): the focus panel: review, live diff, file view and settings; Monaco's background.
- **Card** (card, card-bottom): cards run from card at the top to card-bottom below; graph nodes
  at rest use card at 94%. Agent and model-slot cards carry a 2px top line in their agent token.
- **Raised** (raised): hover on a control or row; a pressed rail button; the decisions chip.
- **Page raised** (page-raised): chips, facts and file rows inside the left panel.
- **Text** (text): primary text everywhere; a slightly warm white, because pure white glares on
  video against black. **Text hover** (text-hover): the primary button's fill on hover.
- **Muted** (muted): secondary text: model name, times, counts, hints.
- **Faint** (faint): tertiary text: a module's function count, text-box placeholders.

### Code colors

Syntax colors are low in chroma, so code never looks like an agent or a state. Monaco starts from
its built-in dark theme (`vs-dark`) with these overrides: plain code syntax-plain, keywords
syntax-keyword, strings syntax-string, numbers syntax-number, built-ins syntax-builtin, a
function's name at its definition syntax-definition, comments and line numbers code-dim. A
highlighted span (the selected function) is span-highlight (white at 4%); a folded region is
fold-tint (white at 3.5%).

### Day mode

The v4 dark surfaces leave the day token set unchanged; day cards keep day-card and inputs day-field.

Day mode exists for the submission gallery, which shows both modes. **The video and the demo are
dark.** Building day mode is a bounded front for Mon Nov 2, started only once level 2 is closed;

- **Every color comes from a token**, including the colors Cytoscape, Monaco and the dot-field

### Named Rules

**The Agent or State Rule.** A color is an agent (orange A, blue B) or a state (green passed or
added, red failed, removed or destructive). Nothing else gets a hue: no brand color, no accent, no
decorative gradient. Counters across agents are neutral.

**The Never Alone Rule.** Every agent color comes with its letter; every state color comes with a
word, a sign (`+`, `-`) or an icon. No state depends on color alone.

**The No Amber Rule.** There are no yellow or amber warnings. A warning is said in words with an
icon, in neutral colors.

## Typography

**Body Font:** Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (with sans-serif)
**Label/Mono Font:** Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono (with monospace)

**Character:** One family built for legibility: Next for text and graph labels, Mono for code,
hashes, money, step and line counts, and any number shown as data. In the review panel only,
Mono is for code, inputs and outputs, paths and hashes; counts inside a sentence stay in Next.
Cost stays in the provenance caption in Next, including "cost unknown"; review chips hold no money.
Standalone line counts, including +2 −0 in chips and the file row, use Next 14/600.
This local narrowing keeps prose readable without changing the agent card's numeric readings or
the dock's counts, durations and times. Both fonts come from Fontsource packages;
Monaco is created after the fonts load, or calls `monaco.editor.remeasureFonts()`.

### Hierarchy

- **Headline** (700, 20px, 1.3): the change's title at the top of the left panel.
- **Brand** (700, 17px): the product name in the top bar.
- **Title** (700, 16px): the agent's name on its card, section headings.
- **Body** (400, 15px, 1.5): running text and buttons. Prose such as the agent's note stays within
  about 75 characters a line.
- **Graph name** (400, 18px): a function's or method's name on its row; its state word is 16px, 600.
  A frame's title is 17px, 600, and its count 16px, 400. The graph is read at 100%, the size the
  sketch is drawn at, so these are the sketch's sizes, not the panels' scale.
- **Label** (600, 14px): buttons, pills, chips and labels that name something.
- **Group** (600, 11.5px, tracked 0.08em, uppercase): group labels such as the queue and settings
  groups; the exception to sentence case.
- **Caption** (400, 13px): captions, keyboard hints, a module's function count.

**The Sentence Case Rule.** Sentence case for titles, actions and prose. Only group labels use

### Graph nodes and their states (signature component)

A node's look answers two questions, each on its own channel, and says its state in a word on the
node ("read", "editing", "changed", "new"):

1. **Is there a change here that is not in main yet?** The border: dashed in the agent's color, or
   not. This channel always wins.
2. **What is the agent doing with it now?** The letter badge: outlined for read, filled for a
   change.

- **Rest:** card fill at 94%, 1px node-border, node radius, name in text at 18px. Hover turns the
  border node-hover. A module frame: panel fill at 90%, 1px card-border, module radius, its name in
  module-label, its function count in faint. An exact call edge: 1.4px in edge, with a small filled
  arrowhead of the same color.
- **Read:** the agent read the node in its current run, and the node has no pending change. Solid
  1.5px border in the agent's color, an outlined letter badge, the word "read" in the agent's text
  color. Fill unchanged.
- **Editing:** the node is in the agent's current focus: the `node_ids` of its latest `agent.focus`
  event, when that event's mode is edit and the agent is working or revising. Only that latest
  focus counts; when the agent moves on, the previous nodes become "changed". Dashed 1.5px border
  in the agent's color, the agent's node fill, a filled letter badge, the word "editing". Rings
  spread from it.
- **Pending (changed or new):** the node carries the agent's change and is not its current edit
  focus. Dashed 1.5px border, dash 6px and gap 4px, in the agent's color; the agent's node fill
  (`-new` for a node the agent added); a filled badge; the word "changed" or "new". An edge the
  change adds is dashed in the agent's color, 2px, with an arrowhead in that color.
- **Precedence:** editing over changed or new over read. A pending node the agent reads again stays
  dashed and keeps the word "changed".
- **Selected:** the node whose diff or file the left panel shows gets a 2px ring in text.
- **Possible call edge:** 0.9px in edge-possible, never dashed. "Thin, grey, never
  dashed" wins over every other edge rule: inside the cone it keeps its width and color at full
  opacity, and a possible call that a change adds is not dashed (its node already is).

**Call visibility.** At every detail level (Modules, Functions and Details), resting cross-file
links show exact calls. When either module is folded, exact calls aggregate by directed module
pair; with both modules open they draw between the visible function or folded class rows. Exact
calls retain their taxi routes, weights and arrows, and quiet-edge until a person or agent lights
them. Calls within one module stay hidden at rest.

A module pair with no exact call in either direction may also show one faint, straight, neutral
1px line without an arrow. It appears only if a possible call survives the built-in name filter;
opposite directions share one resting line. No possible function call returns merely because an
agent reads, edits or changes its endpoint. Opening modules, including automatic agent openings,
Functions and Details all keep this rule.

Hovering or selecting a node reveals its incoming and outgoing calls; a module includes its rows,
and a row also reveals its module's aggregates. Possible calls reveal without the name filter,
as thin, solid gray straight lines with direction arrows. A displayed
cone reveals calls with both endpoints in its seeds and entries; possible lines do not extend the
cone, which follows exact calls only. Review and live diff use the displayed cone. Pending edges
and agent lighting retain their rules; possible calls always stay solid and gray.

Hover requests clear on pointer leave; selection requests clear with selection; cone requests
clear with the displayed cone. Requests combine, so a call remains while another includes it.
An edge under the pointer immediately lifts above the other paths in neutral edge-hover, including
its existing arrow and aggregate count. It changes no width, route, node, camera or focus and
introduces no animation. Folded cards and opaque graph text plates mask straight lines beneath them.
Open frames retain their translucent body fill and stay below their rows during edge hover;
straight lines can show through that fill.

**Letter badges.** Agents are a letter and a color, nothing more: no avatars, beads or mascots. On
a node: a 26px rounded square at the right end of the row, on the name's line, with A or B in badge
type. Filled for
editing and pending: the agent's color with the letter in on-agent. Outlined for read: a 1.5px
border in the agent's color, no fill, the letter in that color. On the agent card and in panel
headings: a small square swatch in the agent's color followed by "Agent A" or "Agent B".

**Distance badges.** A 20px circle filled with text, the distance as a page-colored number, 12px,
weight 700. Neutral: the cone is about the code, not an agent.

**Difference chip.** Current-revision behavior facts put “N of M differ” on a changed node's top
border, 12px from its left edge; unstable-only evidence reads “varied on K of M inputs” in muted,
weight 400. No difference or no comparison means no chip. Two changes share the border in change-id
order, each prefixed by its agent letter in text. Use caption-size (13px), label-weight, mono numbers,
radius-control, a solid border token and ground fill; the text token carries established counts.
Click or Enter toggles one opaque panel popover below the node, right-aligned, at most 440px wide,
clamped 8px inside the stage or flipped above. It shows up to eight 26px input/before/after rows,
marks unstable samples, and states omitted differing inputs and limitations. Escape or a second
click closes it; an outside pointer press also closes it. Only the touched chip receives focus,
and Escape is claimed only while its popover is open. Evidence arrival and replacement never open
it, move focus, nodes or the camera. Hidden, folded and too-narrow nodes show no chip; its pixels
stay fixed across zoom. Counts remain available in review. No animation, new token or agent color.

### Impact cone (signature component)

The cone is level 2. In review and in the live diff, the graph shows the change and its cone, and
everything else steps back.

- The change's nodes keep their agent's look; the agent's color stays on the change and nowhere
  else.
- Nodes in the cone keep full opacity and show a distance badge: 1 for a direct caller, 2 for a
  caller of a caller, and so on.
- Exact edges inside the cone and the change draw in lit-edge, 2px, with a matching arrowhead.
- In review, everything outside the cone and the change dims to 16%, including module frames
  and nodes the agent only read. In the live diff, other nodes and edges dim to 16%, module frames
  to 60%; nodes the agent read in this run keep their full read look while the agent works.
- The cone card on the graph lists the possibly affected functions by distance, with counts, and
  says what the bounds cut, for example "+12 omitted by depth". It is "possibly affected", never a
  complete list. In review, the lead block presents differences, the reach chip summarizes the
  cone's size and distance, and "What was compared" gives two to four lines about measurements
  and their limits. The function list stays on the graph.
- The cone follows exact calls only. On main's graph with the change's nodes and edges applied,
  count distinct non-test callers outside seeds and cone entries that have a possible call into
  either and no exact call into either. Count every possible call, without a built-in name filter;
  this is one hop only, with no further traversal, for live edits and proposals alike.
- Possible callers and bounds belong to the cone card's footer, never the review facts row:
  "+2 possible callers, not followed", "+12 omitted by depth". No question-mark icon or warning color.
  An empty cone says "No exact callers"; with one possible caller, "No exact callers · 1 possible
  caller, not followed". Append only a nonzero count. With no graph, show no possible-callers text.
  Bounds and possible callers stay independent of measurements.
- Each cone row is one line: function, measured word, then "Out of view" at the right for a
  function off camera or inside a folded module. Words are "differs" (text, 600), "same" (muted),
  "not measured" (faint), and "varied" (muted) when results changed between runs. Green never
  means "same". Rows are 28px high; words and "Out of view" are 13px, function labels 14px.
- In level 1, before the cone exists, review dims everything except the change's nodes.
- The cone changes node and edge emphasis; it adds no light source to the LED field.

### Dot field and rings (signature component)

The signal sketch's LED field stays: light follows the box it comes from, rather than making a
round glow. It was brought forward from after level 1 and never blocks a level. The field is one
WebGL2 canvas behind the graph that draws the lamps, the rest contours and nothing else. Missing
WebGL2, a failed draw or a lost context leaves plain ground behind the graph: no lamps, no contour
lines (the fallback that existed before the contours).

- **LEDs:** a canvas of 7px cells under transparent Cytoscape, each a 5px square lamp with a 2px
  gap. A lamp shows one of four levels (off, a third, two thirds, whole), picked by an 8 by 8
  ordered dither as the sketch does: the level is the floor of the cell's intensity (the lights
  added, up to 1) times three plus the matrix cell over 64. Near the core every lamp is whole;
  further out the dither leaves a regular pattern of single lamps, which is the LED character.
  Each light is first held back by its own smooth cut (off under `lamp-cut-low`, free from
  `lamp-cut-high`), so a glow ends on a lamp, not on scattered dust. The color follows the level, all
  from tokens: the first level leans to the deep end of the agent's color (the weaker channels
  scaled by `lamp-ramp-deep`, orange toward deep red, blue toward deep blue), the second is close
  to the agent's own, and the whole level moves `lamp-ramp-hot` of the way to the ink (lighter
  orange or blue on the dark ground, darker on paper). Only a lit lamp is drawn: its square, in the
  light's color. An off
  lamp is not drawn, so the ground and its contour lines show between the lit ones, and a faint
  guide dot every 28px stays. At rest no light reaches anything, so no lamp is drawn (this replaces
  "lamp-off and guide-dot make a faint 28px grid at rest"; a field told no modules, so with no
  contours, still draws its off lamps in `lamp-off`). The contour lines
  fade out under a light by its smooth glow, not by its lamps, so no edge of the light's pool cuts
  them in blocks. The canvas reads rendered boxes; it does not draw graph nodes or edges.
- **Shape and spread:** light follows the outline of each row an agent touches (its current row
  and its last read), or of the folded card that holds it, never the whole module frame: the shape
  follows the work, row by row, and lamps under cards stay hidden. The near and far spreads are about 26px and 72px at 100% zoom, measured
  outward from that box, and scale with graph zoom; the near term decays as an exponential and the
  far one has the long tail 1 over one plus the square of its distance (the sketch's), so the
  halo thins over several rings.
  A light always falls off softly on every side, however faint it has become.
- **Sources:** only each agent's current node at full strength and its last read node at about
  0.4 emit light, in that agent's token, and only while that agent is acting (see Agent light).
  A change waiting on an open inbox entry adds one steady, dimmer light on its node, and a decided
  change one fading light. The last read is one node, not an accumulating history; other changed
  nodes, selected neighbors and the impact cone are not additional emitters. The existing
  eight-source budget is unchanged: current foci first, then last reads, with stable
  slot/agent-id order within each group; excess sources are dropped. A decided change's fading
  light is one more source of its agent's color, counted in the same budget.
  Milestone rings and travelling points feed the lamp accumulator directly, without emitter slots.
  This replaces the earlier Sources restriction “only each agent's current node ... and its last
  read node ... emit light” for finite events (milestone decision, Oct 30; clarified Oct 31).
  Intensities add, but each lamp takes the strongest source's team hue, first source on ties,
  instead of mixing hues.
- **Mask:** the title, legend, zoom controls and every overlay mask the field, including the
  contour lines. Neither the LEDs, the lines nor a travelling light may show through those areas.
  So do the text bands: the header of every open module (its first 54px) or class (44px), padded by
  6px (`header-mask-pad`), and the exact box of a folded card; these hide lamps and lines only. An
  agent's marker and a merge's travelling light are clipped by the overlays alone, as the sketch's
  DOM marker (life.html:80) and its pulse canvas (life.html:219, z-index 40) lie above everything,
  so a marker sits whole on a folded card. Rows are 94% opaque and a folded card hides its own
  lamps, so lines are not seen through them either.
- **Module frames are translucent:** an open module or class takes the panel color at
  `module-opacity`, 0.62 in dark and 0.5 in day, so the lamps around a lit row show through the
  frame's body, as the sketch's frame does. Only its header is masked; the body between rows,
  and the margin at their sides, carries the light. An editing row wears its agent's tint
  (`agent-a-node`, `agent-b-node`: a near-black warm or cool wash in dark, a pale one in day), and
  a lit row's text stays at 4.5:1 or more over the brightest lamp beneath it, which a test checks
  in both themes.
- **Working ring:** an expanding ring in the agent's color reports a working agent. It stops
  when the agent stops working. It is a work signal, not a second pool of ambient light.
- **Milestone rings:** beside the working ring, Ready for review gives one quieter circular ring
  outward in 700ms. Its radius starts at half the rendered row width and travels 150px with
  ease-out power 2.4, amplitude `0.9 * (1-p)`, sigma 5px and a 16px trailing side. Needs you gives
  two inward beats within 700ms, each from 150px beyond that same radius, accelerating with
  power 1.6; amplitude rises from 0.4 to 1 times 1.2 with power 1.4, a 90ms fade-in, sigma 8px,
  a 26px trailing side and 160ms afterglow. Every lamp remains a 7px square through the same
  levels, dither and mask. This replaces the earlier Working ring line “It stops when the agent
  stops working” as a rule for all rings: working rings still stop with work; milestone rings
  finish their finite event independently and never loop (milestone decision, Oct 30/31).
- **Marker:** on each agent.focus, the lettered marker glides to the current node along the edge
  route when one exists, carrying its local light with it. When the agent stops acting the marker
  fades out with its light; the Motion rules set its timing and reduced-motion fallback.

**Agent light.** Colored light means an agent is acting now, and then the light belongs to a change,
in three states (maintainer, 2026-10-29):

1. **Working.** The agent's status is working or revising: today's light, with its ring and its
   marker where the agent reads or edits (current node at full weight, last read at about 0.4).
2. **Waiting.** The change has at least one open entry in the decisions inbox, read from
   `selectDecisionInbox(state)` and never from a rule of the field's own, so it follows the
   selector when its rule changes. The light stays on the change's node, steady and dimmer
   (`waiting-light-weight`, 0.5, against 1 for a working agent), with no motion: no ring, no
   marker, no breathing, and it never pulses, blinks or grows stronger however long it waits.
   Opening the review or clicking the node does not turn it off (the mode only scales it, as it
   scales every light); closing the entries does.
   This replaces the earlier Waiting line “no ring ... and it never pulses, blinks or grows
   stronger however long it waits” for finite events only: the Oct 30 milestone decision,
   confirmed Oct 31, allows one Needs you event with two inward beats. The waiting light itself
   stays still between events; readiness also sends its finite event ring, never an idle loop.
3. **Decided.** Merged (by rules or by the person), approved or rejected: a decision fades only a
   light that exists. The afterlight starts from the light the change had on its node at that
   moment (the waiting level, or a working agent's) and fades out over about 90 seconds (the
   sketch uses `(1 - p)^1.6` for the share left). A change with no light then gets none: a
   rejection of it lights nothing. A merge still sends its pulse (Merge travel) and the fade starts
   when it arrives; one whose change had no light brings its light as the pulse arrives, with
   nothing before it.

No color or pulse anywhere else for this: the header's "Decisions · N" counter stays neutral, and
what changed earlier stays told without color by the rail cards and the node marks. An agent that
has stopped or finished, with nothing open in the inbox, has no light; at rest there is none
anywhere, only the contours and ambient motion that no event causes. The dimmer level is a
theme-independent token, so both token sets carry it. This replaces the earlier rule in this
section that the current node and last read stay lit, at their weights, until the change is
decided, the earlier line that the light fades out with the marker on approval, and (within this
front) the first version of the rule, which gave an agent waiting for review no light at all.

**Rest background.** The ground at rest is gray topographic contour lines derived from the graph:

- one moderate hill per module, centered on its frame; its height grows with the module's callers
  (calls into it from other modules) over a small range, so no hill dominates, plus a few soft
  mounds in empty areas;
- wide spacing between levels: 5 to 7 lines around a hill that stands alone (`contour-step` 0.1
  over hill heights of 0.52 to 0.72), never more than 8; a soft 1px line (whole within a tenth of
  a pixel of its center, gone 1.1px past its edge), and fainter toward the outside
  (`contour-fade`: the outermost line keeps 65% of the alpha); each hill spreads over its frame
  (`contour-hill-spread` 1), so rings show outside big cards;
- they drift slowly and continuously, so a change of shape takes minutes and never waits and
  jumps; under reduced motion they are still;
- gray only: the line color is the `contour-line` token (white at 9% in dark, ink at 8% in day), never
  an agent color and never an accent; text keeps 4.5:1 and never sits on them;
- the field's own mask hides them under overlays; they draw in every mode, behind everything.

**Merge travel.** When a change is merged, a pulse in the agent's color leaves that agent's card,
or that agent's letter when the agents panel is folded into the rail, as in review. Approving from
the review panel starts this same travel. It runs along the graph's edges to the merged node
with a short trail that fades behind it (about 100px: 28px bright, then 72px that fade with the
square of what is left, so no line
stays lit), and arrives. The node's light, which held the level the change had from the decision, then fades as in
Agent light (a rejection sends no pulse; its light fades from the decision). The travel is drawn above
the graph, clipped by the field mask, and never moves the camera, opens a panel or takes focus.
When approval starts in review, focus stays in the review panel.
The travel also lights the LED field: one point at the head and five behind it at 20px steps of
actual path length, including corners and clamped at the start. Each contributes a gaussian
`I = i * exp(-d² / (2r²))`, with `i = 0.95 * (1 - k/6)^1.4`, `k = 0..5`, and `r = 19px`.
At most 12 points enter the common light and color sums before the four-level 8×8 dither and
per-level agent-color ramp; only cells within three radii are visited. The LEDs keep their 7px
square grid and use the drawn lamp masks (overlays and header bands); the SVG is unchanged.
Points stop at arrival; the SVG trail still fades as before. No new idle animation is scheduled.
The Oct 30 milestone decision replaces the earlier “and arrives” endpoint above with a shared
arrival for the travel, card and pending mark, and a finite halo: arrival is exactly the shared 250ms
travel delay plus 1450ms travel, never a separate card or node timer. The card says “Merging”
until arrival, then “Merged”; its node keeps its changed word and pending dashed border until
that instant, when both leave. A 7px band at 40% opacity hugs the node box in the team's token,
contracting to zero over 550ms on the existing masked travel layer. No merged node word is added.
The card has no departure glow or extra merged sentence; there is no 300ms hold before the fade.
Under reduced motion there is no travel or point light: the node light is there at once and fades over the same time.

**What is out of the life layer** (maintainer, 2026-10-29): click waves, a hover flashlight,

**Later in the same front** (target, not yet built): a scan band and one lamp per passing test while

### Class and module folding

Classes and modules fold. At start, every module is one folded card, such as
"pricing.py · 6 functions". A module opens when an agent reads or edits inside it. Clicking a
module's header toggles it open or folded, including after an agent opened it.
Cross-file calls aggregate whenever either endpoint module is folded. A directed pair containing
exact calls retains one routed arrow and counts only its exact calls as ×E. On edge hover or node
selection the label is “×E”, “×E · P possible” for mixed confidence, or “×P possible” for a
possible-only line. Counts retain all underlying calls, including filtered names; labels stay off
at rest and appear immediately, without motion, delay, focus or camera changes.

A possible-only pair has no resting line when its reverse contains an exact call. Otherwise one
faint straight line per unordered module pair survives if any callee name is absent from the sorted
Python 3.12 dir() union of dict, list, set, frozenset, str, bytes, tuple and the public io classes.
The committed generation command lives beside builtinNames.ts. This filter affects only resting
drawing: it never removes graph data, counts, hover/selection calls or cone members. Lines meet the
two cards' borders and pass below opaque cards and their text, never through titles or counts.
The legend's faint straight swatch reads “Possible call, matched by name”. Hover lift uses the
neutral edge-hover token; edge-faint and edge-hover exist in both theme sets.

Classes start as one header row with their method count. A click toggles the class; a fresh change
adding or modifying a method opens it, and the person can fold it again. Reading a hidden method
lights the class row with the read badge. Cone membership projects onto that row at the nearest
distance; calls to hidden methods merge onto the row. Imports remain hidden.

Preset coordinates and fold state determine positions. Only the later rows and modules in the
same column slide when a class toggles, for 240ms with `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.7, 0.2, 1)`; under reduced
motion they jump. Mode changes move the camera alone.

Only module frames drag. Function rows stay in their frame's column. The frame's edges re-route
on every animation frame during the drag, never only on drop.

### Scale and depth

This definition sits beside graph structure and folding because depth controls how much unfolds,
while scale controls how it is drawn. It refines [Layout](#layout)'s **Opening a change** readability
floor, **The preset's arrangement** and fit behavior, together with the folding rules above.
The depth control is built (`bfe7a18`). The map view and folder groups are left out of the current level. They come after the Conversation tab, milestone pulses and life layer part 2, built only from a sketch the maintainer approves (maintainer, 2026-10-31).

1. **Two axes.** Depth is the Modules / Functions / Details control: how much unfolds. Scale is
   the zoom: how it is drawn. Apply the floor in Layout's **Opening a change** rule to module
   titles, measuring the `frame-title-size` token (17px) times the zoom. The graph enters the map
   automatically below that floor: below 64.7% (11/17). It leaves above a slightly larger title
   size, fixed by the sketch, so it does not flicker at the edge. The fit button lands on cards
   when the fitted zoom stays at or above the leave threshold, otherwise on the map. The zoom
   readout shows "Map" beside the percentage. There is no fourth button.
2. **Nothing moves.** Each module becomes a circle at the center of its block, using the space
   already reserved by Layout's **The preset's arrangement**. The circle's area is proportional
   to its function count. Changing level is a crossfade; under reduced motion, a cut.
3. **Folder groups, on the map only.** A group is the first folder level that splits the
   repository into at least two groups; tests are their own group. A group with fewer than two
   modules draws no frame; a flat repository draws no frames. Never computed clusters. Cards
   stay as today. A continuous frame needs each folder's modules placed together: that changes
   the preset layout and also moves card positions. The sketch shows this, and it is part of
   the build decision.
4. **Group summary.** Name and module count; a ring in the color of each team working inside
   (color means a team, per the separate team-color decision); pending decisions, neutral; how
   much of the cone reaches it, for example "3 of 9 in billing".
5. **Lines.** The call-visibility rules
   ([DESIGN.md "Call visibility"](#graph-nodes-and-their-states-signature-component)): exact links
   at today's weight; possible links that survive the built-in name filter, drawn as faint lines.
   Nothing invented. With few lines, the LED field and the contours carry the texture.
6. **Names.** Constant size on screen. Group names always shown; module names where they fit,
   first those an agent touches, the cone reaches or the selection holds, then by size; the rest
   on hover.
7. **Interaction.** Clicking a group zooms to frame it, and the cards come back. Clicking a circle
   brings that module to 100%.
8. **Scope.** The map view and folder groups are left out of the current level. They come after the Conversation tab, milestone pulses and life layer part 2, built only from a sketch the maintainer approves (maintainer, 2026-10-31).
   This definition stays for a later level. Sketch first, with real data from Boltons and the demo,
   and the proof 2 repository if it changes. The proof 2 repository, Boltons `d31d821`, has 66 modules by top
   folder in [its preset](fixtures/layouts/boltons-d31d821.json): boltons 30, tests 32, misc 3,

### Motion

Nothing moves without a cause, with one allowance (2026-10-29): ambient motion and light that no
event causes stay allowed on the graph's ground, and the rest contours are that allowance. Event-driven
transitions take 160 to 320ms with `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.7, 0.2, 1)`; the standard is 240ms,
including module and class opening. Each motion says what changed:

- **Agent starts:** its card slides and fades in from the right.
- **agent.step / usage.tick:** a new step row slides in at the top, older rows move down; the
  step bar and spend number ease to the reported value.
- **agent.status / change.proposed:** the agent card's status pill crossfades; the permanently
  mounted decisions chip updates its label and count. These events never open a panel or move
  keyboard focus.
- **agent.focus:** the lettered marker in the agent's color glides between nodes, following the
  edge route when present, with its light following it. A working agent's status lamp may pulse
  (the existing 1.6s period) and its graph ring expands only while work continues.
- **Module or class opens:** its rows slide into place, including an opening caused by an agent's
  read or edit. This does not rearrange the preset between modes.
- **Person opens review:** the camera eases to the change, the focus panel slides in and the diff
  scrolls to its first hunk. The live diff follows a new edit and marks the changed lines once;
  a proposal does not switch tabs or turn it into review by itself.
- **change.resolved, approved and merged:** pending dashed borders become solid, rows settle into
  main's neutral colors, added nodes grow in, the agent marker fades out, and a pulse runs from the
  agent's card along the graph's edges to its node (about 1.5s, with a short trail that fades
  behind it); the node's light then fades over about 90s, with the soft falloff of every light.
  The pulse never moves the camera or takes focus.
- **Milestone events:** the Oct 30 decision (clarified Oct 31) replaces immediate removal of
  merged pending marks in change.resolved above with removal at arrival. Its 700ms rings and
  550ms node halo are explicit exceptions to the 160–320ms motion range above, on the graph only.
  Same-team milestones less than 1000ms apart coalesce; a merge replaces the ring with its travel.
  Only the ring's own team dips: Ready starts 55% lower and recovers over 500ms; Needs you dips
  44%, then swells 30% on landing and settles through its afterglow. Other teams never dim.
  A second team's ring on the same node starts 350ms later. At most four milestone effects run
  at once: discard Ready first, then Merged; never discard Needs you (defer an excess Needs you
  until capacity is free). None changes camera, selection, tabs or keyboard focus.
- **View changes:** dimming and highlights ease to their new state after navigation or an event. A
  change of view is a plain crossfade, never a wipe.

**Reduced motion** (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`) turns all of these motions off, including
card and row movement, value easing, crossfades, rings, lamp pulses, marker travel, merge travel,
fades, node growth, camera moves and animated scrolling. State and camera changes happen
immediately; the field's current light and the lamp remain still, and the contours are still. One
fade stays, because the maintainer asked for it (2026-10-29): after a merge the light appears at
once, with no travel, and fades over the same 90s, in coarse steps. No information depends on
motion.
The Oct 30 milestone decision extends Reduced motion above: no milestone ring, travel or halo,
and no arrival wait; card and pending marks change immediately. Merges already present when
mounted are history, already arrived, and never replay a pulse. Removing an event key forgets
it, so restarting replay can play that milestone again.

### Quality floor

Each item is a target checked on the built result:

- **Focus:** every control, node and row that takes input has a visible focus ring (2px text color,
  offset 2px). Keyboard order follows the screen; nothing steals focus.
- **Contrast:** text at 4.5:1 or more against its surface; graphics that carry meaning (agent
  borders, badges, dashes, state icons, call edges, the focus ring) and control boundaries at 3:1.
  The dark-set contrast tables below cover the v4 surfaces and card gradient; the day set keeps
  its table above. Decorative borders do not carry a control's identity or state; meaningful
  boundaries use control-edge and focus uses text. Values below the floor stay visible as
  maintainer notes, not silent token changes.
- **Errors:** every error message says what happened and how to go on, in the product's words:
  "The merge conflicts with main in pricing.py. Ask Agent A to redo it on the new base, or reject
  it." Never a bare code or "Something went wrong". Errors use neutral colors with an icon;
  failed red is for failing tests and destructive actions, not for chrome.
- **Browser surfaces:** text selection, caret, scrollbars and focus rings take theme tokens.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** keep every agent color next to its letter, and every state color next to a word, a sign
  or an icon.
- **Do** keep the decisions chip and any count across agents neutral.
- **Do** show a pending change with a dashed 1.5px border in its agent's color, changed and new
  alike.
- **Do** keep the current view when an agent finishes: only its card, its pill and the decisions
  chip report readiness.
- **Do** take every color from a token under `data-theme`, Cytoscape, Monaco and the dot field
  included: hovers, highlights, gradient ends and the grid's lamps and dots too, never a raw hex or
  `white` in a component.
- **Do** check contrast against the quality floor in both modes.

### Don't:

- **Don't** use amber or yellow, for warnings or anything else.
- **Don't** use color alone to say anything.
- **Don't** give any hue a meaning other than agents A–F, passed or failed.
- **Don't** steal focus or open a tab when an agent finishes; motion follows only the causes above.
- **Don't** dash anything but a pending change: not possible calls, selection, hover or empty
  states.
- **Don't** add surface effects beyond the card layers, frosted graph overlays, still matte header and
  the field's contours; no click waves, hover flashlight, pointer-following borders, button flashes,
  boot sweep or wipes.
- **Don't** leave colored light on at rest: only an agent that is acting, a change that waits on an
  open entry of the decisions inbox (steady and dimmer, never moving), or one decided in the last
  90 seconds lights the graph.
- **Don't** move nodes between modes.
- **Don't** give Approve a keyboard shortcut, Ctrl+Enter included.
- **Don't** use uppercase outside group labels, or Mono as decoration.
- **Don't** copy sketch implementation into product code; the approved v4 images are design references.
